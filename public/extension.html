FIVE MINUTES TO YOUR FIRST SHORTCUT

Make a little room in your toolbar.

  1. Download and unzip mimic-browser-companion.zip. Keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the extracted folder.
  3. Pin Mimic from Chrome’s Extensions menu. Click its icon to open the side panel.
  4. Choose Save a reading and run Test with review. Continue the save step when prompted.
  5. For a batch, set the outcome selector to #confirmation and expected text to Saved. Download the practice CSV, upload it, and review the mapped rows.

Already have Mimic installed? Replace the files in its existing folder, then click Reload on its extension card after stopping any active recording or run. Your saved library stays in the same Chrome profile. Export a backup before removing the extension.

Chrome displays a debugging indicator while Mimic controls its dedicated workflow tab. Recording inside embedded frames or new popup tabs is not supported. Keep review checkpoints on actions that submit, send, or change important data.

Privacy notice · September 8, 2026

Mimic is a browser workflow tool made by Aradhya Mishra. This notice covers the Chrome extension and mimic-aradhya.vercel.app.

Workflow data

Recorded clicks, selectors, page URLs, input values, captured text, CSV inputs, and run results are saved locally in your Chrome profile. They are not uploaded to Mimic’s billing server. The extension operates the current tab you explicitly select or a dedicated workflow tab you ask it to open, using Chrome’s Debugger API. It does not record general browsing history. Recognized password and private fields become manual steps; review recordings because other inputs can still contain sensitive information.

When you run a workflow, the inputs are sent to the destination website through the actions you chose. That website’s own policies apply. User-triggered JSON and CSV exports can contain your data; keep them private when appropriate. The experimental website workspaces store their content in that site’s local browser storage.

Accounts, billing, and usage

Account sign-in is provided by Neon Auth. It stores account information and manages passwords and verification. Mimic’s database stores your user ID, email, Stripe customer/subscription references, subscription status, a hashed extension connection token, and monthly batch usage. Usage requests contain an operation ID rather than spreadsheet contents. Extension connections expire after 30 days and can be revoked from the account page.

If paid subscriptions are enabled, Stripe handles hosted checkout, payment details, invoices, and the billing portal. Mimic does not receive or store full card numbers. Vercel hosts the site and account endpoints and may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address and error logs to operate the service. No advertising analytics or tracking scripts are included in this release.

Use and sharing

Mimic uses data only to provide and secure its workflow, account, and subscription features. We do not sell personal data or use browser workflow data for advertising or model training. Service providers process data needed to provide their services. Data may be disclosed when required by law.

Mimic’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and retention

You can export your local library from the workspace. Removing the extension removes its local library; make a backup first. Site workspaces can be removed through browser site-data controls. Revoking your extension connection stops future authenticated requests from that connection. Sign out separately on shared computers.

Account and billing records are kept while you use the service and as needed for billing, security, and legal obligations. To request account deletion, access, or correction, contact odoisveryverygood@gmail.com. Cancel an active subscription in the billing portal before requesting deletion; uninstalling the extension does not cancel billing.

YOUR FIRST FEW MINUTES

Try it. Change it. Make it a habit.

Start with a real example before teaching anything new.

  1. 1

    Watch one task actually run.

    Open Mimic and click See it actually work. It saves a reading in the included practice site and checks the result. Your saved example appears under My tasks.

  2. 2

    Give it something of your own.

    Open Save your first reading. Change the title and link, then choose Run task. Look at the saved result and check the Reading Room.

  3. 3

    Type a few items.

    Choose Run a list, fill in Item 1, and click Add another item. Preview your list, review the batch, and start when you’re ready. CSV import is there if you need it.

  4. 4

    Teach your own small routine.

    Choose Teach a task in the side panel. Do a task on a website you use, pick the visible success result, and save it. Try different details once before running a list.

GOOD TO KNOW

Beside you.
In your browser.

Keep Chrome open while Mimic works. Your tasks stay in this Chrome profile, and the Reading Room keeps its links in this browser. They do not automatically sync to another device.

Start with a simple task in one tab. Some steps pause for your attention. If Mimic is unsure about a result, inspect the website before continuing. A checked result is only as specific as the success message you chose.

Multi-tab tasks, uploads, embedded forms in frames, and CAPTCHA handling are not supported yet. There is no automatic booking, shopping, price monitoring, or background work while Chrome is closed.

Free currently includes three personal tasks and three items per list, plus practice. Paid checkout and Chrome Web Store publication are still being prepared.
